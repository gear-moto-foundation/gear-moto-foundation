## Official four pillars

- **G — Gear:** Protective motorcycle gear for riders who need it.
- **E — Education:** Rider safety, training, skills, and endorsements.
- **A — Assistance:** Practical support, guidance, and connecting riders with resources and services.
- **R — Recovery:** Accident-related medical expenses, financial relief, rehabilitation, and rebuilding after a crash.

The Rider Relief Medical Assistance Program is part of **Recovery**, not the Assistance pillar. Assistance guidance and referrals do not themselves approve or promise grants. Financial support remains subject to board-approved policies, demonstrated need, documented eligible expenses, funding and conflicts controls.

## Publishing and compliance gates

- Do not assert incorporation, approved bylaws, active tax exemption, functioning giving, approved rider assistance, available grants, events, sponsors, or operating results without independent confirmation.
- Do not publish the Rider Relief draft's provisional grant ceilings or accident thresholds as guaranteed benefits or adopted legal rules.
- Public-road accident standards and controlled-course exceptions need counsel and independent board approval before use.
- Do not place applicant medical/financial documents or private records in GitHub or ordinary email.
- Confirm legal entity status, EIN, IRS determination, Utah registrations, insurance, finalized eligibility, donation processing and privacy/consent practices before activation.
- Finance projections and staffing plans are **planning assumptions**, not actual organizational revenue or impact.
- Sponsor recognition is not an endorsement, and donors cannot choose specific beneficiaries.
- The Astro site is still a GitHub source project and is **not live** until built and deployed.
