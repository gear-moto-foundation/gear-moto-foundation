# About GEAR Moto Foundation

## Protect the Ride. Support the Rider.

GEAR Moto Foundation is a developing motorcycle-focused nonprofit initiative built around a straightforward idea: riders deserve access to protective gear, meaningful training, and a community ready to help after a serious crash.

Our intended work connects three priorities: prevention through gear, rider preparation through education, and post-accident support.

## Our approach
We aim to build transparent eligibility rules, accountable fundraising and partnerships, and a rider-first community that values skill, responsibility, and practical support.
