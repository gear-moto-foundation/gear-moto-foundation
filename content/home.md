# GEAR UP. TRAIN SMART. RECOVER STRONG.

**Protect the Ride. Support the Rider.**

GEAR Moto Foundation is being developed to help more people ride with appropriate protection, quality instruction, and a stronger community of support when the unexpected happens.

[Explore our programs](/programs/) · [Get involved](/contact/)

## More than a ride. A community that looks out for its own.

We believe motorcycle safety and support shouldn't end when the engine shuts off.

### Safety gear assistance
Helping remove financial barriers to essential protective gear.

### Rider education
Making rider training, skills development, and endorsement support more accessible.

### Accident recovery
Developing a responsible assistance program for riders facing qualifying accident-related medical expenses.
