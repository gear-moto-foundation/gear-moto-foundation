# Our Programs

## Safety Gear Assistance
Proposed support for eligible riders who need suitable protective equipment. Exact approved item categories, contribution amounts, and eligibility requirements remain under development.

## Rider Education
Proposed assistance for motorcycle safety courses, training and endorsement-related costs, subject to program rules and available funding.

## Accident Recovery Support
A developing program intended to help eligible riders with accident-related medical cost-sharing such as deductibles and out-of-pocket expenses, within funding limits and documented criteria.

**Eligibility under consideration:** A crash involving an at-fault driver, with exclusions for stunt riding or excessive speed and potential distinctions for sanctioned events. These are discussion-stage concepts, not final eligibility rules. Any application, decision, or payment mechanism requires adoption of written policies.

*No program is represented here as currently accepting applications.*
